chapter 01

who i am

hi, i'm lhenna. a ui/ux designer who also dabbles in frontend dev. i got into design because i've always cared about how things feel, not just how they look. that tiny moment when a user just gets an interface without thinking? that's what i'm chasing every single time. i'm currently working on real projects, learning as i go, and slowly building a body of work i'm proud of. ✦

chapter 02

the real me

outside of work, i'm probably gaming, deep in some aesthetic rabbit hole, or being judged by my cat stormi. she's a tortoiseshell and she is, without question, the most important person in my life. i like cozy things, purple everything, and interfaces that feel like a warm hug. i'm a little magastos, a little chaotic, and very much a work in progress. and i think that's okay. oh, and i sleep. a lot. i've been a certified sleepy kid since birth. the kind who can fall asleep anywhere, anytime, no questions asked. 🐱

chapter 03

where i'm going

i used to paint. back when life felt lighter and time moved slower. i haven't picked up a brush in a while, but that same part of me found a new home in minecraft. building worlds block by block, making something beautiful out of nothing. turns out it's not that different from design. honestly? i don't know exactly where i'm headed in life yet. but i know i want to keep making things, keep learning, and keep finding little pockets of joy. whether that's in a pixel, a brushstroke, or a minecraft build. ✦
